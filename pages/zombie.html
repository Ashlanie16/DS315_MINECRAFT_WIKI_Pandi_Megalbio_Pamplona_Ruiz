Zombie

For the underwater version, see Drowned. For the desert version, see Husk.
“Uuurrrgh…”— What most players hear first, a few seconds before a door starts to shake

Zombies are common hostile undead mobs that spawn in the dark in the Overworld. They shamble toward players, villagers, and iron golems and attack in melee. They are slow, durable, and often show up in groups, which makes them one of the first real threats a new player faces.

Zombies burn in direct sunlight unless they are standing in shade, in water, or wearing a helmet. Several related mobs share their basic design, including the baby zombie, the husk, the drowned, and the zombie villager.

Contents

    Spawning

    Zombies spawn in dark areas of the Overworld, such as caves, forests at night, and unlit rooms. They appear in groups and can also come from monster spawners in dungeons. A small share of spawns are baby zombies, and a small share spawn carrying armor or a weapon.

    Lighting your base well is the simplest way to stop them from spawning near your home.

    Behavior

    A zombie tracks a nearby player and walks straight toward them, attacking when it gets close. It will also attack villagers and iron golems, and it can trample turtle eggs.

    • Doors: on Hard difficulty, zombies can break wooden doors.
    • Reinforcements: on Hard difficulty, a zombie that is attacked can summon more zombies.
    • Items: zombies pick up dropped armor and weapons and use them.
    • Daylight: they catch fire in sunlight unless covered, shaded, or in water.

    Combat

    A zombie has 20 health (10 hearts). Its damage depends on the difficulty setting. Pick a difficulty to see how much one hit hurts:

    Quick tips

    • Fight from higher ground, or from inside a one-block-wide corridor.
    • Hit once, step back, and hit again to avoid taking damage.
    • Shields block a zombie’s attack completely.

    Villager infection

    When a zombie kills a villager, the villager may turn into a zombie villager. The chance depends on difficulty: it never happens on Easy, happens about half the time on Normal, and always happens on Hard.

    To cure a zombie villager, hit it with a splash potion of weakness and then feed it a golden apple. After a few minutes it turns back into a villager and gives discounted trades.

    Drops

    • Rotten flesh (0 to 2): can be fed to wolves, traded, or eaten in an emergency, with a chance of causing hunger.
    • Iron ingot, carrot, or potato: rare drops when the zombie is killed by a player.
    • Armor or weapon: if it was carrying one, it can drop on death.

    Trivia

    • A baby zombie can ride a chicken, which is known as a chicken jockey.
    • Zombies are undead, so Smite deals extra damage and a splash potion of healing hurts them.
    • A zombie wearing a helmet will not burn in daylight until the helmet breaks.
