Skeleton

For the Nether version, see Wither skeleton. For the snowy version, see Stray.
“Clack… clack…”— The sound of bones, usually followed by an arrow

Skeletons are hostile undead mobs that spawn in the dark in the Overworld. They are armed with bows and shoot arrows from a distance, so they are dangerous even when you cannot reach them.

Skeletons burn in sunlight unless they wear a helmet, which keeps most of them underground by day.

Contents

    Spawning

    Skeletons spawn in dark areas such as caves and dungeons. They often appear in groups and also come from monster spawners.

    Skeleton horsemen are rare, and they appear during thunderstorms.

    Behavior

    A skeleton keeps its distance from the player and shoots arrows. It strafes left and right to be harder to hit.

    • Sunlight: they catch fire unless covered, shaded, or wearing a helmet.
    • Wolves: skeletons run away from wolves.
    • Arrows: they shoot quickly and can hit from far away.
    • Water: they will not burn in water.

    Quick tips

    • Use a shield and walk straight toward it.
    • Hide behind blocks and attack when it reloads.
    • Smite deals extra damage, since skeletons are undead.
    • Bring a tamed wolf to make skeletons run.

    Drops

    • Bone (0 to 2): used for bone meal and taming wolves.
    • Arrow (0 to 2): used with bows.
    • Bow: a rare drop, which may be enchanted.

    Trivia

    • Skeletons are undead, so a splash potion of healing hurts them.
    • A skeleton can kill a creeper with an arrow, which drops a music disc.
    • Skeletons can pick up items and wear armor, just like zombies.
