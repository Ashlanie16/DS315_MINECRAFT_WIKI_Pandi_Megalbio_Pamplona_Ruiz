Creeper

For the lightning-struck version, see the Variants section.
“Ssssss…”— The last sound many builds ever hear

Creepers are silent green hostile mobs that spawn in the dark in the Overworld. They sneak up on players and explode when they get close, damaging the player and blowing holes in the nearby blocks.

Creepers have no ranged attack and no sound until the fuse is lit, which makes them one of the most feared mobs for builders.

Contents

    Spawning

    Creepers spawn in dark areas such as caves, forests at night, and unlit rooms. They can also come from monster spawners. Unlike zombies and skeletons, they do not burn in sunlight.

    Lighting up the area around your base stops them from spawning near it.

    Behavior

    A creeper walks toward the nearest player. When it gets close it starts to hiss and swell, and a moment later it explodes.

    • Fuse: it explodes about one and a half seconds after it starts to swell.
    • Cancel: if you move away or knock it back, the fuse stops.
    • Cats: creepers run away from cats and ocelots.
    • Blocks: the explosion destroys most blocks around it but not bedrock.

    Quick tips

    • Hit the creeper once and step back to knock it away.
    • Use a shield to block most of the blast.
    • Keep a cat near your base to keep creepers away.
    • Do not fight near your buildings, since the blast breaks blocks.

    Variants

    VariantWhereWhat is different
    Charged creeperAnywhere during a thunderstormStruck by lightning. It glows and has a much bigger explosion.

    Drops

    • Gunpowder (0 to 2): used for TNT and fireworks.
    • Music disc: dropped when a skeleton kills it with an arrow.
    • Mob head: a charged creeper’s blast can make other mobs drop their heads.

    Trivia

    • Creepers were created by accident when a creator mixed up the model of a pig.
    • They are one of the best-known symbols of the game.
    • Creepers are afraid of cats, which gives players an easy way to keep them away.
