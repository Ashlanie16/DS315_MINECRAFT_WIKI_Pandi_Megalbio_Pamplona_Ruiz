Villager

For the infected version, see Zombie villager. For the wandering seller, see Wandering trader.
“Hrmm.”— The sound of a villager deciding it will not sell you a better price

Villagers are passive mobs that live in villages. They have no attack, but they trade items for emeralds, work at job sites, and sleep in beds. Their trades make them one of the main ways to get rare items without searching for them.

Villagers are weak and flee from danger. Iron golems protect them, and zombies attack them on sight.

Contents

    Spawning

    Villagers spawn in villages when the world is generated. More can be born when villagers breed, or they can be created by curing a zombie villager. Their look changes with the biome of the village, and their robes show their profession.

    Behavior

    A villager follows a daily routine. It works during the day, gathers in the village center, and goes to sleep in a bed at night.

    • Fleeing: villagers run and hide from zombies and illagers.
    • Doors: they open doors to move between buildings.
    • Sharing: farmers throw spare food to other villagers.
    • Protection: iron golems defend villages with enough villagers.

    Professions

    A villager with no job can take a profession by claiming a nearby workstation block. Each profession has its own trades. Pick a profession to see its workstation and what it sells:

    Quick tips

    • Trade often to level a villager up and unlock better trades.
    • Do not hit a villager. Others will remember and raise their prices.
    • Keep villagers fenced in and out of reach of zombies.

    Trading

    Right-click a villager to open its trade list. Most trades use emeralds, and a villager levels up each time you trade with it. The levels are Novice, Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, and Master, and higher levels unlock better trades.

    Breeding

    Two villagers breed if they have beds free and enough food, such as bread, carrots, or potatoes. When they are willing, hearts appear and a baby villager is born. A baby grows up into an adult after some time.

    Zombie infection

    A villager killed by a zombie may turn into a zombie villager. On higher difficulties this happens more often. A zombie villager can be cured with a splash potion of weakness and a golden apple, and a cured villager gives discounts on trades.

    Drops

    • Items: villagers do not drop any items when they die.

    Trivia

    • A villager shakes its head when it refuses a trade or has no stock left.
    • Villagers with no profession wear plain brown robes and a blank outfit.
    • Saving a village from a raid earns the player the Hero of the Village effect.
