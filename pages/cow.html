Cow

For the mushroom version, see Mooshroom.
“Moooo.”— The first sound many players hear on the surface

Cows are common passive mobs found in grassy areas of the Overworld. They wander in small herds and never attack. Players raise them for food, leather, and milk, which makes them one of the most useful animals early in the game.

Cows are slow, harmless, and easy to herd by holding wheat. They run away briefly when hurt, but otherwise they are happy to stay near a fenced pen.

Contents

    Spawning

    Cows spawn in groups of two to four on grass blocks in well-lit areas of the Overworld, such as plains, forests, and meadows. They are often found already generated in the world when a new area is created.

    Once a cow is spawned, it stays around until it is killed or until it is left far from all players.

    Behavior

    A cow wanders slowly and stops now and then to graze. It ignores players unless one is holding wheat.

    • Following: a cow walks toward a player holding wheat.
    • Panic: when hurt, it runs in random directions for a few seconds.
    • Fall damage: cows take fall damage like most mobs.
    • Leads: they can be tied to a fence with a lead.

    Quick tips

    • Build a fenced pen so your cows do not wander off.
    • Kill a cow while it is on fire, or cook the beef, for steak.
    • Keep an extra bucket handy for a quick cure from effects.

    Breeding

    Feed two adult cows wheat and they will pair up and produce a baby cow. Players also get a small amount of experience for each birth. The baby grows up in about twenty minutes, and feeding it wheat speeds this up.

    Adult cows need a few minutes to rest before they can breed again.

    Milk

    Using an empty bucket on an adult cow fills it with milk. Drinking milk removes all active status effects, including poison and hunger. Milk is also needed for cake.

    Variants

    VariantWhereWhat is different

    Drops

    • Raw beef (1 to 3): becomes steak when cooked, or if the cow dies while burning.
    • Leather (0 to 2): used to make armor, books, and item frames.
    • Experience: killing a cow gives a few experience orbs.

    Trivia

    • Steak is one of the best early foods because it restores a lot of hunger.
    • Cows are one of the animals that can be bred with wheat.
    • Milk is one of the few ways to instantly clear bad effects.
