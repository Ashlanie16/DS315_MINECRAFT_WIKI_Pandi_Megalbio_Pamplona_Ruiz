Spider

For the smaller poisonous version, see Cave spider. For the mounted version, see Spider jockey.
“Ssskrrt… ssskrrt…”— The sound of eight legs on stone, usually right above your head

Spiders are common hostile arthropod mobs that spawn in the dark in the Overworld. They are fast, can climb walls, and attack in melee. Their large size and red eyes make them easy to spot, but hard to escape once they have noticed you.

Unlike most hostile mobs, spiders become neutral in bright light. A spider in daylight will leave you alone unless you attack it first. They never burn in sunlight, so you will often see them wandering the surface by day.

Contents

    Spawning

    Spiders spawn in dark areas of the Overworld, such as caves, forests at night, and unlit rooms. They can also come from monster spawners in dungeons. A very small share of spawns are spider jockeys, where a skeleton rides on the spider’s back.

    Because they are wide, spiders need a bit more room than zombies to spawn. Lighting up your base still stops them from appearing near your home.

    Behavior

    A spider tracks a nearby player and rushes toward them, then leaps at them to attack. It will not chase you across the world, but it is quick enough that running away rarely works.

    • Climbing: spiders can climb any solid wall, so a tall fence or wall will not keep them out.
    • Light: in bright light, spiders are neutral and only fight back if attacked.
    • Cobwebs: spiders walk through cobwebs without being slowed.
    • Hard difficulty: a spawned spider may come with a random potion effect.

    Quick tips

    • Stand in a two-block-high tunnel so the spider cannot climb over you.
    • Its jump attack is fast, so hit it first as it leaps in.
    • Bane of Arthropods deals extra damage to spiders and slows them down.
    • Fight in daylight if you want to avoid the fight altogether.

    Spider jockey

    Occasionally a spider spawns with a skeleton riding it. The skeleton shoots arrows while the spider runs you down, so you are attacked from two directions at once. Killing the skeleton first leaves a lone spider to deal with.

    Spider jockeys are rare, so seeing one is usually a sign you are in a dark, unlit area.

    Drops

    • String (0 to 2): used to craft bows, fishing rods, and wool.
    • Spider eye (0 to 1): a rare drop when the spider is killed by a player. It is poisonous to eat, but it is used to brew potions.
    • Experience: killing a spider gives experience orbs.

    Trivia

    • Spiders are arthropods, so the Bane of Arthropods enchantment deals extra damage to them.
    • When you spectate through a spider’s eyes, your view gets a multi-eyed filter effect.
    • Spiders are one of the few hostile mobs that do not burn in the sun.
    • Cave spiders are smaller than spiders and fit through one-block gaps.
