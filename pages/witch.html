Witch

For the villager she resembles, see Villager.
“Hee hee hee!”— Her laugh, a few seconds before a potion lands near you

Witches are hostile mobs that live in swamps and attack by throwing potions. They wear a purple robe and a black hat, and they look much like a villager.

A witch drinks potions to heal and protect herself, so she is trickier to kill than most mobs of her size.

Contents

    Spawning

    Witches spawn in swamp huts, where they stay for good, and in dark areas of the Overworld. They can also take part in raids on villages.

    Behavior

    A witch throws potions at nearby players and drinks potions when she is hurt or in danger.

    • Throwing: she throws harmful potions from a distance.
    • Drinking: she drinks potions to heal or avoid damage.
    • Resistance: she takes much less damage from potions.
    • Raids: witches join raids on villages.

    Quick tips

    • Approach from the side so that she misses.
    • Use a bow to attack from range.
    • Bring milk to remove bad effects.
    • Kill her quickly before she drinks and heals.

    Trivia

    • Witches are the only mob that throws potions at players.
    • Witches are not hurt much by potions, so harming potions barely hurt her.
    • Swamp huts are where you will find a witch waiting.
