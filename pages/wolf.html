Wolf

For the tamed version, see the Taming section below.
“Grrr… *woof!*”— A wild growl, followed by a happy bark once it is tamed

Wolves are neutral mobs that live in forests and snowy forests of the Overworld. Wild wolves keep their distance, but they fight back if attacked. A player can tame one with bones to get a loyal companion that follows and defends them.

Tamed wolves wear a collar, can sit on command, and are far tougher than wild ones. They teleport to their owner if left far behind.

Contents

    Spawning

    Wolves spawn in packs of around four in taigas, forests, and groves. They only spawn on grass or snow blocks in the Overworld.

    Once a pack is spawned, they wander together near each other.

    Behavior

    A wild wolf is neutral. If you attack it, the whole pack turns on you and its eyes glow red.

    • Hunting: wild wolves hunt sheep, rabbits, and foxes, and they attack skeletons.
    • Pack: if one wolf is hurt, nearby wolves join the fight.
    • Water: a wolf shakes itself dry after leaving water.
    • Fear: skeletons run away from wolves.

    Taming

    To tame a wild wolf, feed it bones. Each bone has a one in three chance to work, so you may need several. When it succeeds, hearts appear and the wolf gets a red collar.

    A tamed wolf has 40 health (20 hearts), compared to 8 health (4 hearts) for a wild one. Its tail shows how healthy it is: the lower the tail, the lower its health. Pick an item to see how it affects a wolf:

    Quick tips

    • Bring lots of bones. Skeletons are an easy source.
    • Do not hit a wild wolf. The whole pack will fight you.
    • Make a tamed wolf sit before you leave it somewhere safe.
    • Feed a hurt wolf meat to heal it.

    Combat and armor

    A tamed wolf attacks whatever its owner attacks, and whatever attacks its owner. It can be given wolf armor to take less damage, which wears down as the wolf is hit.

    Breeding

    Feed two tamed wolves meat and they will produce a baby wolf. The puppy is already tamed and grows up over time.

    Drops

    • Items: wolves do not drop any items.
    • Experience: killing a wild wolf gives a few experience orbs.

    Trivia

    • Wolves are one of the few animals that can be tamed and will fight alongside you.
    • A tamed wolf’s collar is red by default and can be dyed any color.
    • Angry wolves have glowing red eyes.
