Wither

For the weaker mob that is found in Nether fortresses, see Wither skeleton.
“Hrrrrrnnnn…”— The low sound it makes as it powers up

The Wither is a hostile boss mob that a player has to build. It has three heads, flies, and shoots exploding skulls that give the Wither effect. It is one of the toughest mobs in the game, and defeating it gives a nether star.

It attacks almost every mob nearby, not only the player. It destroys most blocks around it, so it is best fought in an open or protected area.

Contents

    Summoning

    To summon a Wither, place four blocks of soul sand or soul soil in a T shape, then put three wither skeleton skulls on top. The skulls come from killing wither skeletons in a Nether fortress.

    • The last skull you place starts the summoning.
    • The Wither’s health bar fills as it charges up.
    • When the bar is full, it explodes and then starts to fight.

    Behavior

    The Wither flies around and attacks nearby mobs. It does not attack undead mobs such as skeletons and zombies.

    • Skulls: it shoots black skulls from its side heads, and sometimes a rarer blue skull that is stronger.
    • Wither effect: being hit turns your hearts black and drains your health.
    • Blocks: it breaks most blocks it touches, but not bedrock.
    • Flying: it hovers above the ground and can follow you across the area.

    Quick tips

    • Summon it in a deep tunnel or underground room with a low ceiling.
    • Bring milk to clear the Wither effect and golden apples to heal.
    • Use a strong sword with Smite to deal extra damage.
    • Use a shield to block some of the skulls.

    Strategy

    Most players fight it in a small tunnel so that it has to come close. Beacons are the main reward, so many players build a Wither farm once they can beat it.

    Drops

    • Nether star (1): used to craft a beacon.
    • Experience: about 50 experience orbs.

    Trivia

    • The Wither is one of the two bosses in the game, alongside the Ender Dragon.
    • It is undead, so instant healing hurts it and instant damage heals it.
    • Its health bar appears at the top of the screen during the fight.
