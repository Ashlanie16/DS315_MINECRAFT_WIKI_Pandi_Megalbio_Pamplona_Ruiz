Ender Dragon

For the creature that lives in the same dimension, see Enderman.
“Rrraaawr!”— The roar that tells you the fight has started

The Ender Dragon is a hostile boss mob that lives in the End. It is the only boss that is found in the world from the start, and defeating it is a major goal for many players. It flies, spits acid, and heals from tall crystals placed around the island.

Killing the dragon opens a portal back to the Overworld and drops a large amount of experience. It also leaves behind a dragon egg the first time it is defeated.

Contents

    Spawning

    The Ender Dragon is present when a player first enters the End. It spawns once, and it can be summoned again by placing four End crystals around the exit portal. A fight on a world where the dragon is already dead gives much less experience.

    Behavior

    The dragon flies around the main island and changes its attacks as the fight goes on. It can also destroy most blocks it flies into.

    • Crystals: End crystals on obsidian pillars heal the dragon over time.
    • Breath: it spits purple acid that leaves a lingering cloud on the ground.
    • Charging: it dives at players and knocks them back.
    • Perching: it lands on the exit portal and rests for a short time.

    Quick tips

    • Destroy the End crystals first, starting with the ones in iron cages.
    • Bring a bow, many arrows, a shield, and blocks to hide behind.
    • Wear armor and bring food and a water bucket in case you fall.
    • Strike the head while the dragon is perched for the most damage.

    Strategy

    Most players first use a bow to destroy the crystals and then fight the dragon when it lands. Beds, when they explode in the End, deal heavy damage to the dragon and are a common shortcut.

    Stand away from the edge of the island. A fall into the void is the most common way to lose the fight.

    Rewards

    When the dragon dies, a portal back to the Overworld opens at the center of the island, and a dragon egg appears on top of it. A gateway also appears that leads to the outer End islands.

    Drops

    • Experience: about 12,000 for the first kill, and about 500 for later kills.
    • Dragon egg: a one-of-a-kind block that appears only after the first kill.

    Trivia

    • The Ender Dragon is one of the two bosses in the game, alongside the Wither.
    • Its health bar appears at the top of the screen during the fight.
    • The dragon cannot destroy obsidian or bedrock.
