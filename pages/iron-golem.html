Iron Golem

For the snow-based golem, see Snow golem. For the copper golem, see Copper golem.
“Thud… thud… thud…”— The footsteps of a village’s protector, heard before it is seen

Iron golems are large, tough neutral mobs that defend villages. They attack most hostile mobs on sight and hit so hard that their targets are flung into the air. They can also be built by players to guard a base.

An iron golem does not hurt players unless the player attacks it, attacks a villager, or has a bad reputation in a village. It never despawns and can be repaired with iron ingots.

Contents

    Spawning

    Iron golems spawn naturally in villages that have enough villagers. They appear near the village center and keep guard there. A golem spawned by a village is called a village golem.

    A player can also build one. A player-built golem will never attack its creator.

    Building

    To build an iron golem, place four iron blocks in a T shape: one block on the ground, one above it, and one on each side of the middle block. Then place a carved pumpkin or jack o’lantern on top.

    • The blocks and pumpkin turn into a golem as soon as the head is placed.
    • There must be a free two-block space under the arms of the T.
    • The cost is 36 iron ingots, since each iron block is made of 9 ingots.

    Behavior

    An iron golem walks slowly and stays near its home. It attacks hostile mobs that come close, with the exception of creepers, and it swings its arms to launch them into the air.

    • Villagers: a golem may hand a poppy to a child villager.
    • Players: a village golem becomes hostile if you hit a villager or the golem itself.
    • Zombie sieges: golems rush to defend the village when zombies attack.
    • Water: golems are heavy and sink, so keep them away from deep water.

    Combat and damage

    An iron golem has 100 health (50 hearts). Its attack deals between 7 and 21 damage on every difficulty, and it knocks its target high into the air. As it is damaged, cracks appear on its body. Pick a damage stage to see how much health is left and how many iron ingots it takes to repair:

    Each ♥ above stands for 10 health.

    Quick tips

    • Never fight a village golem. It is much stronger than most players early on.
    • Use an iron ingot on a damaged golem to heal it.
    • Do not attack villagers if you want to stay on friendly terms.
    • Build one next to your base entrance to keep hostile mobs away.

    Drops

    • Iron ingot (3 to 5): the main drop, and a good source of iron.
    • Poppy (0 to 2): drops from the flowers it carries.

    Trivia

    • Iron golems have no real weakness and are one of the strongest mobs that players meet early on.
    • The vines on its body are decoration only and have no effect on how it fights.
    • Iron golems are one of the few mobs that give a flower to a villager.
