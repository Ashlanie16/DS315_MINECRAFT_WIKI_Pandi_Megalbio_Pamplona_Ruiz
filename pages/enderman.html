Enderman

For the boss that shares the same dimension, see Ender Dragon.
“vwoop… vwoop…”— The sound of a teleporting Enderman, usually right behind you

Endermen are tall, neutral mobs found in the End, the Overworld, and the Nether. They teleport and can pick up and carry blocks.

An Enderman stays calm until a player looks at its head. Then it turns hostile, screams, and teleports to attack.

Contents

    Spawning

    Endermen spawn in dark areas of the Overworld, in the warped forest of the Nether, and in large numbers in the End. They usually spawn alone or in small groups.

    Behavior

    An Enderman is calm until provoked. It can be angered by direct eye contact or by being hit.

    • Teleport: it teleports away when hurt and avoids projectiles.
    • Blocks: it picks up some blocks and puts them down elsewhere.
    • Water: it takes damage from water and rain and teleports away.
    • Height: it cannot reach players hiding under a two-block ceiling.

    Quick tips

    • Wear a carved pumpkin when you need to pass one safely.
    • Fight it from a two-block-high tunnel.
    • Look at its feet, not its head.
    • Use water around you to make it teleport away.

    Drops

    • Ender pearl (0 to 1): used for teleporting and for eyes of ender.
    • Experience: a few orbs when killed by a player.
    • Held block: any block it was carrying.

    Trivia

    • An Enderman’s eyes glow purple and turn wider when angry.
    • Endermen are one of the few mobs that can pick up blocks and carry them.
    • Eye contact is the main way to anger one, so look away.
